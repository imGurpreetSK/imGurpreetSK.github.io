I am appalled by the sheer number of people choosing to use LLMs to generate word-vomit responses for simple questions instead of conveying an idea succinctly. It's particularly frustrating when this is done by a colleague in a slack message or email.  

I can very clearly see your response is not yours and is spat out by an LLM. What's worse is all this is done without any fact checks or prior context.

Don't do this. Don't make a machine impersonate you - it impacts your credibility and makes me not reach out to you for any help or discussions.

Alternatively, at least have the decency to reword the response and add your own flare.
